Item 23-F F.4 TRAMO automatic-fit assembly corpus.

Probe: Scratch23f.java is the Scratch23r manual-driver probe (see
../tramo-kernel-rounds/metadata.txt for the dual-driver design and the
identity gates) EXTENDED with the fit-level capture surface the tramo_fit
unit consumes:

  "KER <id> resolved ..."     the resolved TramoSpec scalars every stage
      reads (transform fct, va/pcr/tsig/pc/cancel, tol, ub1/ub2, the mean
      test flag, autoMethod) plus the "amth <NAME>" auto-method witness
      token appended to the resolved line (UNUSED/FTEST/WALD/BIC/AIC);
  "fitbuild ..."              the builder output the pre-stage consumes
      (orders, mean, log, nvars, miss, misspos, the interpolated series
      bit row);
  "fitdec ..."                the jar TramoSeasonalityDetector verdict on
      the RAW series (Qs/Fr statistics and p-values as raw doubles, the
      score, toint), the 23-B cross-check target;
  "fitll ..."                 the LogLevelModule side-echo (a FRESH kernel
      over the same built description: testSeasonality then the builder
      chain logPreference(Math.log(fct)).estimationPrecision(intermediate
      Precision).seasonal(ctx), log/level/slog raw bits, choosingLog, the
      log correction, the resulting description log flag);
  "fitseam <idx> diff|arma|scan ..."  the module ENTRY states (orders,
      mean, outliers, pass, round, regarima().getObservationsCount(), the
      scan curva) - what the module saw on entry, as opposed to the kr
      seam facts' post-states;
  "fitresid <idx> spc <s> nobs <n> <count> <bits...>" the arma-seam
      hand-over: the comespa gate readout and RegArimaUtility.olsResiduals
      (public static, javap-pinned to ArmaModule.process offset 160) as
      raw bit rows;
  "fitcl/fitcoef/fituc/fitdy" the ConcentratedLikelihoodWithMissing views
      of the FINAL estimation (dim, nx, nmiss, ssq, logDeterminant,
      isScalingFactor, k = getArimaSpec().freeParametersCount(); all-
      Coefficients; the unscaledCovariance() upper triangle; the
      differenced response), emitted once per case for BOTH driver arms;
  "rfinal/mfinal ... parcov"  the ParametersEstimation.getCovariance upper
      triangle on the final-model dumps - the ARMA-tail covariance surface,
      captured to witness its separation from the coefficientsCovariance
      regression block (register D9); "cov" remains the regression-block
      surface (the D9 replay target).

Case grammar as in F.3 ("KER <id> <alias+flags [key=value]> <freq>
<startY> <startM>" + "values ..." lines), with the F.4 flag extensions
+nomean (MeanSpec.none()), +easter (EasterSpec test), +fs (transform
None), amth=<UNUSED|FTEST|WALD|BIC|AIC>. gen_cases.py regenerates
cases.txt (35 cases): the sc_*/mr_* planted-outlier series copied verbatim
from ../tramo-outlier-rounds/cases.txt, the airline series from R
datasets::AirPassengers (airline.txt), and the two deterministic boundary
series documented in gen_cases.py (nearur near-unit-root LCG series; the
constant-100 series the pinned engine aborts on - its row is the
capture-level failure witness).

Run recipe: ./run.sh cases.txt <tag> [witness]; one JVM per case,
G2 intrinsic-disable recipe (_dexp/_dlog), -Xss16m; witness mode adds
-Xlog:class+load per case aggregated to <tag>.classload.log.

Gate results (2026-11-23, OpenJDK 21.0.11, jdplus jars 3.9.0):
  fit-capture-1.out == fit-capture-2.out == fit-witness.out byte-identical
  (double capture + witness-mode capture); 35 identity rows: 34 all-1 and
  the pinned fit_const row (events 0 / final 1 / all 0: both arms throw the
  identical DStatException "Too many iterations in search procedure" before
  any round event, the rfinal dump still bit-identical).
  Witness gate on fit-classload.log: ZERO jdplus.spec.x13 loads; the only
